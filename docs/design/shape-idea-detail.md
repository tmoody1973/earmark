# Shape brief — idea detail (`app/ideas/[id]/page.tsx`)

Status: **approved by Tarik, 2026-10-16** (M4-T1). Visitor mode: **Read** (with a few actions). Inherits the garden's world (`shape-garden.md`, PRODUCT.md "Visual direction"); craft bar for this page is **Readwise Reader**.

## Job and audience
Tarik opens one idea — usually because it bloomed — to see how it grew and read the one-page draft Earmark wrote, then decide: use it (newsletter issue, radio segment, product brief), keep it growing, or dismiss it.

## Outcome and proof
- He understands the idea's history in one screen: what it is, how many times it came up, when, and what each mention added.
- He can read the draft comfortably on a phone and act on it.
- Truths: mention count and dates come from real mentions; the timeline's dates are written by code, the notes by the model; excerpts ≤ 25 words; the draft's quotes are limited (enforced in M2-T7).

## Layout (phone first, top to bottom)
1. **Header:** the plant at its stage (large, the page's one illustration), the idea's title in the serif, stage label ("Bloomed · 3 mentions · first heard Oct 6, last Oct 18"), Top of Mind pin.
2. **Living timeline:** one line per mention, oldest → newest, sorted by date ("Oct 6: first heard — a CRM for public radio"). Each line expands to its ≤25-word excerpt and source (Bee conversation · voice note · photo · song). Unconfirmed mentions marked.
3. **The one-pager** (when bloomed): reading mode — comfortable measure, the serif for headings, real typographic hierarchy; format chosen by Earmark (newsletter pitch · radio segment rundown · product brief), shown as a small label. Actions: copy, open in Notion, mark "used".
4. **Backlinks:** related ideas, recommendations that fit, merge suggestion — each with accept / remove.
5. **Footer actions:** dismiss (stamps the idea dismissed, never deletes), forget everywhere (M3-T11, with confirmation).

## States
Compost (1 mention: no draft, a quiet "two more mentions to bloom"), sprouting (2), bloomed without draft yet (draft generating: calm progress line), bloomed with draft, drafted, used, dismissed (greyed, restorable), unconfirmed.

## Constraints
WCAG 2.2 AA; reading measure ~60–70 characters; works at 320 px; no invented content; first names only.
