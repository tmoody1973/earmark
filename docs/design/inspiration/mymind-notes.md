# UX inspiration: mymind (from Tarik, 2026-10-16)

Input for M4's `/impeccable shape` round. The screenshots themselves are mymind's work and stay out of the public repo (`docs/design/inspiration/*.png` is git-ignored); this note describes them in our own words.

## What Tarik shared

1. **Item detail** — a large preview of the saved thing on the left; a side panel on the right with the title, "1 minute ago · source", a **TLDR** box (an AI summary), auto-generated **tags** as chips (plus "+ Add tag"), a **notes** field, and three quiet icon actions at the bottom (mark complete, share, delete).
2. **Everything grid** — the search field *is* the hero ("Search my mind…", very large, italic); tabs **Everything / Spaces / Serendipity** top right; a slim vertical rail on the left (wordmark + a few icons); a masonry grid of mixed cards (quick note, video, checklist, image-led cards) with a short caption under each.

## What Earmark borrows (structure and behaviour)

| mymind | Earmark |
|---|---|
| Search-as-hero | Memory-cue search as the garden's front door — "the place Ann mentioned at dinner" (FR-36) |
| Everything / Spaces / Serendipity | The whole garden · Smart Spaces (FR-32) · Serendipity (FR-34) — already in the PRD |
| Mixed-shape card grid + caption | The shelf of type-aware cards (FR-33); caption = "from Ann · Oct 12" |
| Detail panel: TLDR · tags · notes · actions | Rec/idea detail: the "why" context summary (FR-37) · the Spaces it fell into · living timeline (ideas) · actions *tried / send thanks (receipt) / forget everywhere* |
| Quiet left rail | Desktop navigation; collapses on phone (phone-first) |

## What it does NOT decide

The visual world. mymind's look (warm cream ground, italic serif display, orange accent) is one of the defaults Impeccable's calibration warns about; unless Tarik pins it as a brand commitment, M4's direction round chooses Earmark's own look from its public-radio / record-store world, and borrows mymind's structure only.
